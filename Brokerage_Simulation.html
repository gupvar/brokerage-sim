📦 The Broker's Desk: Strategy & Operations

Welcome to the Logistics Management Simulation. You are a freight broker in Georgia managing a 3PL desk.

🎓 Learning Objectives:
  • Market Dynamics: Understand Headhaul (Outbound from Hubs) vs. Backhaul (Return trips) pricing.
  • Modal Selection: Match freight to equipment (Reefer, Flatbed, Power Only).
  • Risk Assessment: Evaluate "Carrier Scorecards" (Service vs. Cost).
  • Margin Management: Balance Gross Margin % against On-Time Performance (OTP).
🎮 How to Play:
  1. Analyze the Load: Look at the Mode (e.g., Reefer needs cooling) and the Lane Type.
  2. Select a Carrier:
    • Green Score: Expensive but reliable.
    • Red Score: Cheap but high risk of breakdown/claims.
  3. Power Only: Very cheap, but requires a carrier with compatible power units.
  4. Win Condition: Keep your Reputation > 50% and maximize Profit.
